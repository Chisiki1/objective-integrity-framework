# High Assurance Profile

Use for public release, external writes, production operations, privacy, safety, financial, long-running, shared-resource, or recovery-sensitive work. Add blind challenge, read-only independent review, pre-action eligibility, post-action readback, and measured effect episodes without replacing the requested outcome with assurance work.

## Required Records

- Objective contract.
- Open deliverable ledger.
- Evidence map.
- Public or operational threat model.
- Scenario and interaction ledger with boundary-state continuity and workload progress coverage.
- Semantic recomposition map for decomposed checks.
- Correction authorization and change-impact record.
- Whole-candidate structural, final, and pre-action audit receipts when applicable; reuse unchanged evidence rather than repeating mini-release audits.
- External action eligibility projection.
- Effect episode for completed changes or public/external writes.
- Hash-chained durable objective state with stale-writer rejection and explicit partial or unknown effects.
- Transition-admission binding for material candidate-bearing corrections and actions.
- Source/candidate/consumer continuity and workload-progress composition where the outcome depends on transferred state or long-running progress.
- Provenance-compiled skill facts and application/effect binding when a selected skill is material to the action.
- Condition-amendment lineage when a user-authored condition changes.

## Exit Standard

Apply the [whole-scope sequence](../docs/whole-scope-work.md): full required implementation and integration before formal stage checks, safe complete findings collection before grouped repair, then source-bound acceptance. Higher assurance strengthens required evidence, not arbitrary acceptance criteria or mandatory model/delegation quotas. Preserve first faults and action-relative authority during every phase.

Exit when the source-bound outcome, mandatory acceptance criteria, release or external-action eligibility, and post-action readback are satisfied within their evidence limits. A scoped unresolved claim holds only the dependent operation or completion statement unless its harm and reachability require a broader disposition.
