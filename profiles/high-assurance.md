# High Assurance Profile

Use for public release, external writes, production operations, privacy, safety, financial, long-running, or recovery-sensitive work. Add blind challenge, independent audits, pre-action eligibility, post-action readback, and measured effect episodes.

## Required Records

- Objective contract.
- Open deliverable ledger.
- Evidence map.
- Public or operational threat model.
- Scenario and interaction ledger with boundary-state continuity and workload progress coverage.
- Semantic recomposition map for decomposed checks.
- Correction authorization and change-impact record.
- Early, final, and pre-action audit receipts when applicable.
- External action eligibility projection.
- Effect episode for completed changes or public/external writes.

## Exit Standard

Exit when the source-bound outcome, mandatory acceptance criteria, release or external-action eligibility, and post-action readback are satisfied within their evidence limits, or when the exact blocker and minimum unblock condition are known.
