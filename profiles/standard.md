# Standard Profile

Use for normal implementation, repair, migration, review, and project-local operations. Maintain objective contract, evidence map, scenario coverage, implementation snapshot, partitioned checks, and final receipt.

## Required Records

- Objective contract.
- Open deliverable ledger.
- Evidence map.
- Scenario and interaction ledger for material risks.
- Correction authorization for candidate-bearing fixes.
- Audit receipt when a review can change a decision.
- Effect episode for completed changes.

## Exit Standard

Exit when the source-bound outcome and mandatory acceptance criteria are satisfied within the profile's evidence limit, or when the exact blocker and minimum unblock condition are known.
