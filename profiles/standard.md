# Standard Profile

Use for normal implementation, repair, migration, review, and project-local operations. One same-conversation primary owner maintains durable objective state and performs authorized work; independent review remains read-only toward its target.

## Required Records

- Objective contract.
- Open deliverable ledger.
- Evidence map.
- Scenario and interaction ledger for material risks.
- Correction authorization for candidate-bearing fixes.
- Audit receipt when a review can change a decision.
- Effect episode for completed changes.
- Durable objective journal and `objective.txt` projection when the work can resume, compact, queue, retry, or outlive one turn.
- Action-start/outcome/reconciliation records for material or potentially uncertain effects.
- Skill selection and application records only when reusable guidance changes a real decision.

## Exit Standard

Exit when the source-bound outcome and mandatory acceptance criteria are satisfied within the profile's evidence limit. When blocked, preserve the minimum unblock condition and exact return step without turning the blocker into the objective.
