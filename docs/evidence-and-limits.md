# Evidence and Limits

This project uses positive, evidence-aware language.

Recommended wording:

- "designed to preserve the requested outcome"
- "supports proportional evidence"
- "measured status"
- "structural pass"
- "runtime evidence required"
- "adoption guidance"
- "consumer-observed result"
- "identity-bound application"
- "measured in the adopting environment"

Avoid wording that claims universal prevention, guaranteed correctness, complete safety, or empirical superiority without data.

State a material boundary once, near the decision it informs. Limitations should help a reader choose the right evidence route. They should not dominate the introduction or turn the project into a list of negatives.
