# Workflow Transition Admission

1. Build one receipt matching [transition-admission-contract.md](references/transition-admission-contract.md). Source-wide implementation uses v3 with the same owner scope/state as the parent and child dispatch; v2 remains for ordinary non-product boundaries. Legacy v1 is compatibility for an explicitly existing split-task lease only. Older schemas do not prove source-wide selection.
2. For a binding correction or material action, bind exact source authority or independent refutation, causal readiness, frozen S0 and interaction IDs, planned U0, omission consequence, expected objective delta, cost and rework counterfactual, and exact return step. A proposed fix without these inputs remains diagnostic or advisory.
3. The same-conversation owner consumes the decision without treating self-control as independent review. Preserve evidence on a dependent hold; a role label never supplies missing source or closure.
4. Before diagnosis or repair, query current project and shared learning sources for the present signature. Retry a known family only with a changed premise and discriminating prediction.
5. Before a finalized selected-skill action, bind selected bytes, script, planned application, and outcome capture. Append actual execution, result, and effect after the action. Selection and reading are not use.
6. Refresh action-relevant external state immediately before a released action. Stale or unknown state replans only that action.
7. Wait at decision windows, reusing unchanged cursors and evidence. Fixed polling and narration without a decision delta are not admitted.
8. Bind one primary owner/work-unit/resource identity or one bounded child lease with non-overlapping resources. Independent reviewers remain read-only. Reconcile old leases and unknown effects before migration.
9. Preserve scenario recomposition and the final consumer target. Bind semantic locks, relational claims, witness, unresolved relations, consumer claim IDs, and oracle for a material edit.
10. Reassess allocation only when job shape changes. Bind the actual allocation input, result, identities, and accepted configuration; caller labels are insufficient.
11. After the episode, record objective delta, consumer result, avoided and induced rework, status-only cost, recurrence, scenario omission, and role violations as observed, unavailable, or not applicable. Feed a lifecycle revise, merge, narrow, retire, or no-change candidate without self-activation.

Run `python -B scripts/transition_admission.py --input <receipt.json>`. The validator closes enumerated identity, reference, set, and status relations only. It does not prove source fidelity, causal correctness, scenario completeness, impact safety, skill semantics, freshness truth, waiting value, or final outcome.

V3 consumes the sibling lifecycle `work_phase.py` before lightweight routing. `EDIT` requires `IMPLEMENT` or grouped `REPAIR_FINDINGS`; construction feedback cannot reclassify external apply, push, CI or an accepted-product regression as a cheap probe. Keep phase and action-authority checks separate, retain raw/partial/unknown earlier effects when the next action is held, and continue independent safe work.
